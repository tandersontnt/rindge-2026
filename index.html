About this page

Summary, not legal advice. Compiled from public sources: town agendas and minutes, state statutes, census and tax data, news reports, and a small number of clearly labeled unofficial sources. This is not an official town record. Vote tallies come from minutes text and should be checked against the linked PDFs. Before relying on any item, verify it with the Rindge Town Clerk, the Planning Department, Town Counsel or the NH Department of Revenue Administration.

  • The deadline to file for exemptions (elderly, disabled and others) was April 15, 2026, per the town calendar in the Annual Report. Official

  • The only related 2026 article was the veterans' disability tax credit increase to $4,500 (Article 12 on the final warrant; numbered Article 11 in the Jan 7 draft). It passed, per press reports. OfficialPress-reported

How exemptions are set under state law

  • RSA 72:39-b: towns set separate exemption amounts for ages 65–74, 75–79 and 80+. Minimums: income limits of at least $13,400 single / $20,400 married; asset limits of at least $35,000 (excluding the residence); each exemption at least $5,000. State law
  • RSA 72:39-a: "net income from all sources"; three consecutive years of NH residency before April 1; assets exclude the residence and up to the greater of 2 acres or the minimum lot size. State law
  • RSA 72:27-a: adopting or changing an exemption requires a town meeting vote, with the article placed on the warrant by the Select Board or by voter petition (RSA 39:3). The vote must state the exemption's provisions and amounts. State law

Petitioned warrant articles in general

  • RSA 39:3: a petitioned article needs the signatures of 25 registered voters or 2% of registered voters, whichever is less (never fewer than 10). The Select Board may make only minor textual changes. State law
  • Rindge is an SB2 town. Under RSA 40:13 II-a, petitions are due the second Tuesday in January: Tue Jan 12, 2027 (the preceding Friday, Jan 8, for bond articles). Statutory, not confirmed by the town
  • At the Deliberative Session, articles may be amended but not to eliminate their subject (RSA 40:13 IV(c)). State law

Deliberative Session timing. The Town Clerk's elections page describes the session as typically the first Saturday of February, but the 2026 session was Sat Jan 31, the earliest legal date. The 2027 date (between Jan 30 and Feb 6) has not been posted.
Conflict

Election results source. The town's results page (news item T2_R44) returned 404 on Oct 9. The Ledger-Transcript and Keene Sentinel report the same tallies; the Sentinel lists the budget as 824-707 (no-yes).

Conservation Commission position on 75 ft. "Opposes" appears only in an automated summary. Official minutes show concerns and a consultant review, but no vote or position.
