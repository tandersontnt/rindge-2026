Glossary

SB2 (official ballot town meeting)
Rindge holds town meeting in two parts. At the Deliberative Session voters discuss and can amend articles. A few weeks later everyone votes by ballot on the second Tuesday of March. RSA 40:13
Deliberative Session
The first session, held on a Saturday between the first and second Saturdays after the last Monday in January. Amendments are allowed, but they cannot remove an article's subject. RSA 40:13, III–IV
Warrant article
A question on the town ballot. The Select Board places most articles; 25 registered voters (or 2%, if fewer) can petition one onto the warrant. RSA 39:3
Default budget
The budget that applies if voters reject the operating budget: last year's appropriations, adjusted for debt service, contracts and legal obligations, minus one-time spending. Rindge went to default in 2011, 2021, 2025 and 2026. RSA 40:13, IX(b)
Capital reserve fund (CRF)
Money voters set aside for a future purchase, such as a truck or the revaluation. The fund's article says what it can be used for. Plain-language summary
ZBA (Zoning Board of Adjustment)
Five elected members serving 3-year terms. Hears appeals of zoning decisions and decides variances and special exceptions. Any action needs 3 concurring votes. Zoning Ord. Art. XV; RSA 674:33
Variance
ZBA permission to depart from the zoning ordinance. The applicant must meet all five tests: not contrary to the public interest, the ordinance's spirit is observed, substantial justice is done, surrounding values are not diminished, and enforcement would cause unnecessary hardship. Valid for 2 years unless used. RSA 674:33
Special exception
A use the ordinance allows only if the ZBA finds the listed conditions are met: no excessive traffic, noise or odors, no harm to nearby values, adequate septic, water and parking, and the town's attractiveness preserved. Zoning Ord. Art. XV.B
Frontage
The length of a lot along a public road maintained by the town or state, or in an approved subdivision, "to which the lot has legal right of access". Rindge requires 250 ft in every district. Zoning Ord. Art. XX #17
Class VI road
A public way the town does not maintain, including roads discontinued and made subject to gates and bars. RSA 229:5, VII
Conditional Use Permit
A Planning Board permit for uses the ordinance allows only under conditions, such as short-term rentals or home-based businesses in the Res-Ag district. Zoning Ord. Art. XII; RSA 674:21
3/5 vote
Bond articles need 60% yes, not a simple majority. Several Rindge articles have won a majority but still failed this test. As printed on Rindge bond ballots (see Trends)

How exemptions are set under state law

  • RSA 72:39-b: towns set separate exemption amounts for ages 65–74, 75–79 and 80+. Minimums: income limits of at least $13,400 single / $20,400 married; asset limits of at least $35,000 (excluding the residence); each exemption at least $5,000. State law
  • RSA 72:39-a: "net income from all sources"; three consecutive years of NH residency before April 1; assets exclude the residence and up to the greater of 2 acres or the minimum lot size. State law
  • RSA 72:27-a: adopting or changing an exemption requires a town meeting vote, with the article placed on the warrant by the Select Board or by voter petition (RSA 39:3). The vote must state the exemption's provisions and amounts. State law

Petitioned warrant articles in general

  • RSA 39:3: a petitioned article needs the signatures of 25 registered voters or 2% of registered voters, whichever is less (never fewer than 10). The Select Board may make only minor textual changes. State law
  • Rindge is an SB2 town. Under RSA 40:13 II-a, petitions are due the second Tuesday in January: Tue Jan 12, 2027 (the preceding Friday, Jan 8, for bond articles). Statutory, not confirmed by the town
  • At the Deliberative Session, articles may be amended but not to eliminate their subject (RSA 40:13 IV(c)). State law

    Revise?

Known gaps and conflicts

Where sources disagree, and what was not available, as of Oct 9, 2026.

Conflicts between sources

Conflict
Forristall Rd variance (Case 2026-12). The ZBA's Sept 22 draft minutes show the variance granted 4-1. On Oct 7 the Select Board voted 3-0 to request a rehearing (from meeting audio; unofficial). Neither the ZBA nor the Select Board has posted a record of the request yet.
Conflict
Default budget amount. The official warrant gives $5,654,177. The Ledger-Transcript reported $5.64M (and $5.65M in January); the Keene Sentinel reported $5.7M. This site uses the warrant figure.
Conflict
Draft vs. final 2026 warrant. The Jan 7 draft had a $25,000 Revaluation CRF (pulled Jan 21), a $150,000 Highway CRF (the final article was $185,000), a Land Use Change Tax share of 20% (amended to 99% at the Deliberative Session), and different article numbers (the veterans' credit was Art. 11 in the draft and Art. 12 on the final warrant).
Conflict
Deliberative Session timing. The Town Clerk's elections page describes the session as typically the first Saturday of February, but the 2026 session was Sat Jan 31, the earliest legal date. The 2027 date (between Jan 30 and Feb 6) has not been posted.
Conflict
Petition deadline. The general RSA 39:3 "fifth Tuesday" rule would give Feb 2, 2027, but SB2 towns follow RSA 40:13: the second Tuesday in January (Jan 12, 2027). The town has not posted this date.
Conflict
Election results source. The town's results page (news item T2_R44) returned 404 on Oct 9. The Ledger-Transcript and Keene Sentinel report the same tallies; the Sentinel lists the budget as 824-707 (no-yes).
Conflict
Conservation Commission position on 75 ft. "Opposes" appears only in an automated summary. Official minutes show concerns and a consultant review, but no vote or position.
Conflict
2023 and 2024 budget tallies. The 2023 and 2024 annual reports both print 497-256 for the operating budget, and 2024 Articles 5 and 9 (zoning Amendments 4 and 8) both print 666-237. These tallies are withheld here until the Town Clerk confirms them.
Conflict
2018 Article 13 (250th Anniversary trust fund). Unresolved. The typed minutes show a 715-715 tie (a tie fails), but a handwritten recount note on the ballot page reads 711-707. Later records show the fund existed.
Conflict
Elderly married income limit. The 2025 MS-1 report prints $49,000 (the figure set by the 2012 petition); the 2020 MS-1 printed $50,000. Single ($35,000) and asset ($150,000) limits match.
Conflict
2021 elderly exemption article. The 2021 report prints the original Article 19 wording (lowering the limits). The Deliberative Session had amended it into a study committee, and the 748-376 vote was on the study article.
Conflict
2023 zoning vote date. The 2023 report gives March 14, 2023; the Wetlands Ordinance cover and the amendment list say March 28, 2023.
Conflict
2016 Deliberative Session date. The 2016 report (scan) says Sat Jan 30, 2016; an unofficial copy of the minutes says Sat Feb 6, 2016.
Conflict
2025 default budget. Select Board minutes and the 2025 report give $5,487,882; the Ledger-Transcript reported $4.49M. The Select Board later found errors and filed an amended default (Nov 12, 2025).
Conflict
2025 revaluation increase. The town's reassessment page gives an average residential increase of 89.6%; Select Board minutes cite about 96.7%. They may measure different things.
