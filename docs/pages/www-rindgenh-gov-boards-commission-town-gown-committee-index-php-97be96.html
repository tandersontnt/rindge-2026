Town & Gown Committee

The Town & Gown Committee’s Vision: Rindge is a community of people who live, work, and learn together in a positive, synergistic way on a daily basis. 

The Town & Gown Committee’s Mission: The mission of the Rindge-Franklin Pierce University Town Gown Team is to ensure a vibrant working relationship and reliable communication for the betterment of all and to have a direct line of communication between the Organizations.
