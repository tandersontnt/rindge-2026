Recycling and Transfer Station

Attention - The Transfer Station hours will be adjusted on Friday, June 26th, to be open from 1:00 pm to 3:00 pm due to the America's 250th Celebration and Parade.

TRANSFER STATION STICKER PRICE

For the sale of April 16, 2026 - April 15, 2027 Dump Stickers

Due to the rising costs of managing the Transfer Station, the Board of Selectmen has had to raise the cost of the Transfer Station Stickers (Dump Stickers). The cost will be as follows:

$50 per year - Regular Dump Sticker

$25 per year - Senior Dump Sticker - For residents over the age of 65

**Please note that only ONE senior sticker is allowed per year, per household.**

Dump stickers can be purchased at the Town Clerk's Office or the Selectmen's Office, 30 Payson Hill Road during normal business hours.

** Please Note: STICKERS ARE NOT AVAILABLE AT THE TRANSFER STATION.
