2025 Reassessment

To view your updated property value, follow these instructions:

  1. Click this link, which will bring you to the Avitar database.
  2. Click Guest, then enter the Challenge Phrase and click login.
  3. Scroll to Rindge, and click the link. This will bring you to the Rindge database.
  4. In the top left, click the dropdown button that says Searches and Select Property Address or Owner Name if you do not know your Parcel ID/Map and Lot.

Both the property addresses and the owner's names are organized alphabetically. Find your property and click to view.

Call 603-899-5181 ext. 100 for assistance if you have trouble finding your property.

View the Avitar's Taxpayer FAQ page before calling with concerns about your assessment. This page is a great resource for generic questions about why your assessed value increased, how assessed values are determined, and why the Town cannot answer all your questions.

Please be aware that the Town Office employees are unable to make any changes to your property card if you find any errors. Town Office employees were also not involved in calculating your assessment, so they cannot inform you why specific changes were made to your property values. Any specific questions regarding your assessment will need to be directed to Avitar. 

You can call 603-899-5181 ext 113 to speak with the Town's Assessing Clerk, Jennifer Helsel, to schedule an appointment with an Avitar employee. Her office hours are Wednesdays and Thursdays from 8:30 am - 4:30 pm. Please leave a message with your name, number, and brief message if you call outside of her office hours, and she will get back to you.
