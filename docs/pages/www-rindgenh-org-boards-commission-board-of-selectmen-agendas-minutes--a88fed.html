Board of Selectmen Agenda List

This is where you can find the Public Notices, Agendas, Minutes, and Video links for the public meetings of the Board of Selectmen. 

If you would like to see agendas and minutes from before 2024, a records request can be submitted at the Town Offices to the Executive Secretary or Town Administrator. When your records request is submitted, you will receive a response to inform you of the timeline that your request will be fulfilled. This timeline will depend on the scope of the request and the difficulty of acquiring the documents. Once the documents are prepared, we are happy to provide you with physical copies of the documents you are seeking or send the documents as PDFs to you via email.
