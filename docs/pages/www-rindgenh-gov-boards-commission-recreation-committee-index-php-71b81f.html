Recreation Committee

About

The Recreation Committee meets regularly, alternating between the first Monday and Wednesday of each month. If you would like to get something on the agenda, you must submit your request in writing to the Recreation Director or Committee Chair one week prior to the meeting date.

To obtain copies of Recreation Committee minutes, please contact the Recreation Director
