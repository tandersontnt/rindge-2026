Mission
“The mission of the Rindge Police Department is to foster collaborative efforts and partnerships, endeavor to protect life, property, and constitutional rights of all and pursue justice with compassion and respect for all our citizens to ensure a safe and secure community. It is our obligation to provide quality law enforcement services in a fair and impartial manner, consistent with the rights and dignity of all citizens, as provided for by the law under the Constitution of the United States and the State of New Hampshire. It is essential that we hold ourselves accountable to the highest ethical standards while striving to be proactive in obtaining and preserving the trust of the community we serve. To accomplish this mission, a set of core values must be met: Integrity, Excellence, Leadership, and Community. We will serve our community with respect and dignity.”
