Building Department

Building Permit and Inspection Process

PLEASE NOTE: Personnel respond to fire/medical emergency calls when necessary. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

Inspections
Inspection requests must be submitted no less than 24 hours prior to the desired inspection date and time. Advance notice is required to ensure scheduling availability and timely completion of inspections. Please request an inspection by calling the office and leaving a voicemail or calling/texting the building inspector's cell phone at 603-969-3354. Do not request inspections via email, as we cannot guarantee that they will be seen in a timely manner.

Inspections occur during the hours of 8:00 am and 10:00 am, Monday through Thursday. Life Safety Inspections will take place on Mondays as needed. Office hours are listed on the left hand side. Thank you!

**Please note that permit applications are reviewed weekly on Wednesdays at 10:00 am.
